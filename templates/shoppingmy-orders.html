Cancellation & refund policy

You can request cancellation before an order is picked up for delivery. Requests are unavailable once an order is picked up, out for delivery, or delivered.

Every request is reviewed by LocalMart. Once approved, captured online payments are refunded to the original payment method; your bank or payment provider controls when the credit appears. Uncaptured payments are cancelled without a refund.
